Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Smart Ghar ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Smart Ghar, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password (when you sign up)
  • Profile Information: Phone number (optional), profile picture (optional)
  • Financial Data: Expense entries, budget information, khata (ledger) records, rashan (grocery) lists, and bill information that you enter into the app
  • Photos: Images you attach to transactions, bills, or payments (optional)

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version
  • Push Notification Token: For sending you reminders about bills and backups
  • Crash Reports: Anonymous data to help us fix bugs

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Smart Ghar app
  • To authenticate your account and secure your data
  • To send you bill due reminders, backup reminders, and monthly reset notifications
  • To improve the app and fix bugs
  • To respond to your support requests

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Where Your Data is Stored

  • Locally on Your Device: Most of your financial data (expenses, khatas, rashan lists, bills) is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. We do not have access to this data.
  • Firebase (Cloud): Your account credentials (email) are stored securely on Google Firebase servers for authentication purposes.
  • Backup Files: If you create a backup, it is encrypted with AES-256 and stored on your device. You may share it via your preferred method (WhatsApp, Google Drive, etc.).

4.2 Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • AES-GCM encryption for backup files
  • PBKDF2 key derivation with 100,000 iterations
  • Firebase Authentication for secure login
  • HTTPS for all network communications

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly choose to share a backup file
  • Service Providers: Google Firebase (for authentication and push notifications)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law

6. Third-Party Services

Smart Ghar uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config
  • Google Sign-In: Optional login method

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

7. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: To attach photos to transactions and bills (optional)
  • Storage: To save backup files and photos
  • Notifications: To send bill reminders and monthly alerts
  • Internet: For authentication and push notifications

All permissions are optional and requested only when needed.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (it's on your device)
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export your data using the backup feature
  • Opt out of notifications from app settings

To delete your account, go to Profile → Logout or contact us.

9. Children's Privacy

Smart Ghar is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. Data Retention

Your data is retained on your device for as long as you use the app. When you uninstall the app, all locally stored data is deleted. Firebase account data is retained until you delete your account.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
